Recommended Tourist Itineraries

Optimized routes designed for smooth transit and maximum cultural experience.

09:00 AM - 09:45 AM
🚄 Arrival at KTX Andong Station

Take KTX-Eum from Seoul Cheongnyangni (1 hr 50 mins). Take Bus #210 or Taxi to Hahoe Village.

10:30 AM - 01:30 PM
🏡 Explore Andong Hahoe Folk Village

Stroll among Joseon Dynasty hanok houses, visit Mansayeong, and cross river ferry to Buyongdae Cliff.

02:00 PM - 03:00 PM
🎭 Catch Hahoe Mask Dance (Talchum) Performance

Enjoy the live comedy & satire mask dance drama performance at Hahoe Performance Hall.

05:30 PM - 07:00 PM
🍲 Feast on Andong Jjimdak at Gu-Sijang Market

Head downtown for authentic spicy soy braised chicken with glass noodles & local cold beer or Andong Soju.

07:30 PM - 09:00 PM
🌙 Romantic Night Walk at Wolyeonggyo Wooden Bridge

Cross the glowing timber bridge, watch reflections on Nakdong river, and ride an illuminated Moon Boat!

Getting to & Around Andong

Simple directions from major cities like Seoul, Busan, and Daegu.

🚆

From Seoul via KTX-Eum

Board at Cheongnyangni Station (청량리역). Travel time is just 1 hour 50 minutes direct to KTX Andong Station.

🚌

Express Bus (Seoul / Busan)

From Seoul Express Bus Terminal or Busan Central Terminal to Andong Bus Terminal (approx. 2.5 hours).

📱

Essential Apps in Korea

Use KakaoMap or Naver Map for bus routes. Use Kakao T for easy taxi hailing without speaking Korean.
